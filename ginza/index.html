Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Tempura Kondo seats guests in set times: lunch at 11:30 and 13:30, dinner at 17:00 and 19:30.

  • Tsukiji shops open early and most close between 13:00 and 16:00. Sanokiya is closed on market holidays.
