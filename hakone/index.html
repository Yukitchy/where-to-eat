Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Shinsho Soba is closed on Wednesday. Gyoza Center is closed on Wednesday and Thursday.
  • Ashinoko Terrace is closed on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of the month, and hands out numbered lunch tickets in order of arrival.
  • Lake Ashi places are a bus ride from Odawara or Hakone-Yumoto. The ones in Gora and Chokoku-no-Mori are on foot from the train stations.
