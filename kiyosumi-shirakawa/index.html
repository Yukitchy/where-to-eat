Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Many coffee shops here close early, some at 17:00. Sunday Zoo is open Friday to Sunday only, and Fukadaso is closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
  • Arise Coffee Roasters, Arise Coffee Pattana and Kraft Coffee are closed on Monday or Tuesday.
