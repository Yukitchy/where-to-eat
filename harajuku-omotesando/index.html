Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Hours and last orders are as listed on Tabelog on 2026-11-06. Weekend and holiday hours differ at several places.
  • Rosokuya, Gyukatsu Motomura, Spontini and Kura Sushi are popular and can have a line at meal times. Go at opening time or off-peak.
  • Yamacho (black udon) and Matsunaga (soba) are weekday-lunch only and close on weekends.
