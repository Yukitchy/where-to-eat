Weekdays 11:00 to 21:00, weekends and holidays 11:00 to 15:00 (closes when the curry sells out). Irregular days off. 2 min from Ochanomizu Station (JR)

Open in Google Maps →

Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Several counter shops here are cash only and use a ticket machine: you pay first, then hand over the ticket.
  • Kanda Matsuya is closed on Sundays and Mondays. Hinoya Curry closes when the curry sells out, so go earlier in the day.
