Ramen from across Japan

SHIN-YOKOHAMA RAMEN MUSEUM

新横浜ラーメン博物館

A food theme park of ramen shops from across Japan, in a recreated 1958 streetscape. Entrance fee applies; mini bowls let you try more than one shop.

Weekdays 11:00 to 21:00, weekends and holidays 10:30 to 21:00 (last order 30 min before closing)5 min walk

UOKURA SHIN-YOKOHAMA

海鮮と日本酒 うお蔵 新横浜店

Seafood izakaya with sake from across Japan, open until 5 in the morning. Private rooms, three minutes from the station.

Mon to Fri 17:00 to 05:00, Sat, Sun and holidays 13:00 to 05:003 min walk

Peking duck, Chinese

HOUKOUEN PEKING DUCK SHIN-YOKOHAMA

芳香園 新横浜 北京ダック専門店

Peking duck specialist a few minutes from Shin-Yokohama Station, with private rooms and set courses.

Daily 11:00 to 23:00 (last order 22:30)3 min walk

Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Manchinro Honten and Araiya Bankokubashi are closed on Mondays. Ikkaku Yokohama is closed on Wednesdays.
  • The Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum charges an entrance fee, and each ramen shop inside takes orders until 30 minutes before closing.
  • Shin-Yokohama and Chinatown are about 25 minutes apart by train, via Yokohama Station.
