Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Keirin is closed on Tuesday. Momotaro is open until 04:00 and Keirin until 03:00, so both work for a late meal.
  • Most of the list is open on Sunday evening. Hinai and Yakiniku Go open at 14:00 on Sunday, and Yakiniku Go takes its last order at 22:00.
  • Necchuya is in the basement of Arca East, connected to the station. Iseya has private rooms only.
