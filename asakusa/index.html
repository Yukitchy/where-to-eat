Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Yoshikami is closed on Thursday, and Matsunami closes the evening session at 21:00 on holidays.
  • Torikoji is closed Monday and the third Tuesday. Sakura-nabe Nakae is closed Monday. South Park is closed Monday.
  • Toe Library Parc has irregular days off, so check before you walk over.
