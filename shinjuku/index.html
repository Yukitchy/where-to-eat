Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Fuunji and Udon Shin draw long lines at noon. Go at opening time or after 14:00.
  • Hototogisu closes when the day's portions sell out, and is closed Sunday and Monday.
  • Ichiran is open 24 hours and you order on a paper form, so it works at any hour and with no Japanese.
